This is a classic marble race, to be 3D printed. It consists of a square base, cylinder support beam in the middle and many, many tracks. 

Built for halflife by hackclub, a 10-week program where you can design and build hardware projects free of cost!

# Overview: 

First, a little funnel at the top that leads straight into a staircase where the marble falls into another bigger funnel where it will spin around until into falls into a very long and curvy track (it looks like one big sweep, but is made of an S-shaped track, a U-shaped track, and another U-shaped track facing the other way!). Finally, the marble ends its journey by landing into a circular container where you can pick it and roll it down again as many times as you want.
